# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Sargam, his family, and people who discover or receive a project link.
The primary users use iPhones. Most repeat visits open saved project URLs;
the homepage is an occasional directory.

## Product Purpose

sargam.design is a practical home for personal tools, not a portfolio or
marketing site. Visitors recognize a project and follow its permanent link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Launch with a public, indexable homepage and a genuine zero-project state.
- Add compact icon/name shortcuts only when real projects are published.
- Expect roughly one to six projects; use two columns on phones.
- Preserve an owner-controlled order and same-tab navigation.
- Keep public metadata and branded share previews free of private information.
- Keep the homepage statically exportable and independent of hosting-provider
  APIs. Vercel is the initial host; GoDaddy continues to manage the domain.
- Do not build tools, authentication, offline storage, or install prompts here.
  The travel tool is explicitly deferred to a separate effort.
- Existing sibling projects must remain untouched.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied monochromatic monogram beside sargam.design, with a small
optically sized mark, Manrope typography, and a restrained cobalt accent.
Use the white monogram on cobalt for the site's favicon and touch icon.

Keep "My little toolkit.", "Projects", "Nothing here just yet.", and
"Made by Sargam." The tone is warm, personal, and understated.

Follow system appearance automatically, including a true-black dark canvas.
Allow subtle playful icon movement only during real interaction, never on
arrival or while idle. Respect reduced motion.

The Airbnb reference means approachable typography, clarity, and careful
spacing, not copied branding, a booking interface, or image-heavy cards.

## Evidence on Hand

- The user owns sargam.design through GoDaddy.
- The black and white SVG monograms were supplied by the user.
- Manrope is locally bundled with its license.
- No project is approved for launch listing yet.
- No private documents or passwords are included in this application.

## Product Principles

- Useful, direct access comes before showcasing the maker.
- Represent availability honestly.
- Preserve familiar ordering and durable URLs.
- Public presentation must never expose private content.
- Keep the first release small and portable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, comfortable
touch targets, text resizing, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
